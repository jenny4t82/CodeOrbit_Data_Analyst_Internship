# Retail Sales Analysis Report

## Introduction

This report summarizes a Pandas analysis of the provided Retail Sales Dataset from Kaggle.

## Dataset description

The dataset contains 1,000 retail transactions and 9 columns: `Transaction ID`, `Date`, `Customer ID`, `Gender`, `Age`, `Product Category`, `Quantity`, `Price per Unit`, and `Total Amount`. Dates run from 2023-01-01 through 2024-01-01. All columns have 0 missing values, and there are 0 duplicate rows.

## Key metrics

- Total sales: **$456,000.00**
- Average transaction value: **$456.00**
- Transactions: **1,000**
- Product categories: **3**

## Main findings

- Electronics generated the highest category sales at **$156,905** across 342 transactions.
- Clothing generated **$155,580** across 351 transactions.
- Beauty generated **$143,515** across 307 transactions.
- Female customers accounted for **$232,840** across 510 transactions, while male customers accounted for **$223,160** across 490 transactions.
- May 2023 was the strongest month at **$53,150**.
- September 2023 was the lowest month at **$23,620**.

## Conclusion

The dataset shows total sales of $456,000 with Electronics narrowly leading the product categories. Female-customer sales were higher than male-customer sales, and monthly sales varied considerably, with the highest recorded month in May 2023 and the lowest in September 2023.
